🏈 Dice Football

Roll, assign dice to your position groups, beat the defense. Score a touchdown in one drive.
🏈
How to play

1. Roll 5 dice. Tap dice to mark them, then reroll those once (optional).

2. Pick Run or Pass. Drag each die onto a group (or tap a die, then a group). A group's strength is the sum of its dice. Dice on groups your play doesn't use are wasted.

Run (steady): yards = ((OL − DL) + (RB − LB)) ÷ 2. Big plays are rare, no sacks.

Pass (boom or bust): yards = ((QB + WR) − DB) ÷ 2 + (OL − DL) ÷ 3. Big plays are common and huge, but you can be sacked, throw it incomplete (coverage ≤ 0), or get intercepted.

Four downs to gain 10 yards. You start on your own 25. A turnover ends the drive.
